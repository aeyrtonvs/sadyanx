## Reply to whoever asked

This task came from a Slack message (it is in the task above). When you finish with status
"done", also write a reply to that person in your result's `reply` field:

- Only the body, written in $language. The harness adds the greeting ("$greeting") and the
  sign-off ("$sign_off"); do not write either.
- Plain Slack formatting: short paragraphs, a **bold** title per topic, `-` bullets, numbered
  questions for things they must answer or decide. Answer what they asked first, then what you
  did, then what is left for them. At most $max_chars characters.
- Never include secrets, credentials, account ids, internal file paths or command output, nor
  links to your report (it is private to your user). Keep to what they need from you.
- Do not send it yourself: your user reads it and decides whether it is sent. Leave `reply` out
  when the task ends "blocked".
